<div style="max-width: 1050px; margin: 0 auto 60px auto; line-height: 1.8; color: #333; font-size: 1.05rem; text-align: justify;">
  
  <h2 class="section-title" style="margin-top: 10px; text-align: center;">Reframing Research as Optimization</h2>
  
  <p style="margin-bottom: 20px;">
    At the core of our autonomous discovery initiative is the realization that many complex materials research challenges can be fundamentally reframed as optimization problems. By carefully constructing and deploying reward-based systems, our group is building a broad, versatile suite of automated optimization workflows capable of accelerating materials discovery.
  </p>
  
  <p style="margin-bottom: 35px;">
    A critical aspect of this paradigm shift is our transition from traditional <strong>myopic</strong> (short-sighted, grid-based) measurements to <strong>non-myopic</strong> active learning. Rather than passively scanning an entire sample, our algorithms intelligently predict and target the most valuable regions to explore next. This reward-driven approach maximizes information gain, minimizes experimental time, and allows us to actively steer the instrument toward specific physical states.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #1d2a56; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 18px; font-size: 1.25rem;">Our Core Optimization Workflows:</h3>
  <ul style="list-style: none; padding: 0; margin: 0; font-size: 1rem;">    
    <li style="margin-bottom: 15px; display: flex; align-items: flex-start; gap: 12px;">
      <i class="bi bi-check-circle-fill" style="color: #CC6600; margin-top: 4px; font-size: 1.1rem;"></i> 
      <span><strong>Instrument Optimization:</strong> Automated tuning, alignment, and parameter optimization for complex instruments, including both STEM and SPM.</span>
    </li>    
    <li style="margin-bottom: 15px; display: flex; align-items: flex-start; gap: 12px;">
      <i class="bi bi-check-circle-fill" style="color: #CC6600; margin-top: 4px; font-size: 1.1rem;"></i> 
      <span><strong>Combinatorial Libraries:</strong> High-throughput, non-myopic exploration and mechanical/structural mapping of structured combinatorial material libraries.</span>
    </li>    
    <li style="margin-bottom: 15px; display: flex; align-items: flex-start; gap: 12px;">
      <i class="bi bi-check-circle-fill" style="color: #CC6600; margin-top: 4px; font-size: 1.1rem;"></i> 
      <span><strong>Random Libraries:</strong> Rapid property screening and phase discovery across complex, unstructured random material spaces.</span>
    </li>    
  </ul>
</div>
